Anatomy of a motion prompt

A motion prompt that lands names how things move (easing and timing), how one state or shot becomes the next, what the camera does, and the overall style. Here is what that sounds like for three different jobs. Tap any phrase to jump to its card.

  • Give numbers for UI"300ms, cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)" survives the trip into code. "Smooth and snappy" does not.
  • Split camera from subjectIn video prompts, say what the camera does and what the subject does in separate phrases.
  • Ask for lessOne motion per shot, slowly, beats three moves at once. Video models warp when asked to do too much.
  • Name what stays stillFaces, text and horizons drift unless you say they shouldn't.
