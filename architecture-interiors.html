Anatomy of a place prompt

A believable place is built in layers: a style, what it's made of, the space you're standing in, and what's around it. Here are three sentences built that way. Tap any word to jump to its card.

  • One style, then materials“Desert modernism in board-formed concrete and travertine” beats three style names. Materials do most of the work in an image.
  • Put the camera in a roomName the space and one element (“a reading nook under clerestory windows”) so the model knows where you are standing.
  • Add the tellsStyle names are shortcuts. The tray adds each card's Spot it traits, so models that ignore names still get the look.
  • Describe architects, then build new“In the manner of Barragán” works best with the traits spelled out, for a new building rather than a copy of a famous one.
