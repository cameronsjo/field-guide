Anatomy of a type prompt

A type prompt that lands names a face (or a family of faces), how two of them pair, how the text is set, and one treatment that gives it character. Here is what that sounds like for three different jobs. Tap any word to jump to its card.

  • Name the class, then a font"A Didone serif like Bodoni" gives Claude a style and a fallback. The class still works when the exact font isn't available.
  • Ask for free fontsHelvetica, Futura and Gotham are licensed. Asking for "Google Fonts only" gets you something you can actually ship.
  • Give sizes, not adjectives"18px body at 1.6 line height, 65 characters wide" beats "readable". Numbers survive the trip into code.
  • In images, quote the wordsImage models spell what you put in quotation marks and describe the look better than they recognize font names.

Type by mood

Start from the feeling you want and read across: a face for headlines, one for text, a detail that reinforces it, and a whole look to point at. Every name links to its card.
