Illustration & Art Movements

Anatomy of an art-direction prompt

A strong art direction names a tradition, a way of making, a way of seeing and sometimes a painter to channel. Here is the kind of sentence that describes a screen-print poster of a sailboat heading into a low sun, in art-history terms. Tap any word to jump to its card.

  • One tradition, one technique“WPA poster, woodcut texture” gives Claude two clear decisions. Five movement names cancel each other out.
  • Add the tellsA style name is a shortcut. The Spot it line on each card lists the traits, and the tray adds them for you, so models that ignore names still get the look.
  • Describe living artists, don't name themMany image tools block living artists’ names, and borrowing traits is fairer anyway. Every artist with a card here is a historical figure.
  • Say which style leads“An Art Nouveau layout with ukiyo-e flat color” blends well because one style sets the structure and the other only flavors it.
