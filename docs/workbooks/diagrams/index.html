Anatomy of a diagram prompt

A diagram prompt that lands names the question the picture answers, the kind of diagram, the level of detail, the notation and the tool, and one or two mistakes to steer clear of. Here is what that sounds like for five common jobs. Tap any term to jump to its card.

  • Say the one question"How does a payment get from checkout to the bank?" picks the diagram type for you. A diagram that answers three questions answers none.
  • Name the audienceAn exec, a new hire and an on-call engineer need different levels of detail. Say who will read it and what they already know.
  • Fix the levelKeep one level of abstraction per view: context, containers, or components. When it gets crowded, ask for a second view instead of a bigger one.
  • Pick the tool up front"Mermaid I can paste into a GitHub README" or "D2 for our docs site" decides the syntax, the layout engine and what is even possible.

Question to diagram

Start from what you need someone to understand. Read across from the simplest picture that works, to what you reach for when it grows, to the mistake people usually make.
