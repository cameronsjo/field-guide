One screen, every state

Most screens are designed in their happy state and written in none of the others. Pick a screen and read across: the same feature on its first day, while it loads, when a search finds nothing, when it fails, offline, and when you're done. Tap a label to open its card.

The swap list

The quickest edits in product writing. Each line is a phrase that turns up in almost every first draft, what to write instead, and the card that explains why.

Anatomy of a copy request

A request for product copy that comes back usable names the screen, the moment the person is in, the components that need words, the principles to hold to, and what to cut. Here are three. Tap any word to jump to its card.

  • Describe the moment, not the screen"Someone's card was declined while they were checking out a gift for tomorrow" gets better copy than "payment error screen".
  • Give the real nounsPaste your product's actual names for things (projects, boards, spaces) and ask for one term per concept. Models drift into synonyms otherwise.
  • Ask for a string tableRequest keys, strings and character counts in a table. You get copy you can paste into code and a check on length in one go.
  • Ask for every stateName the empty, loading, error and success states up front, or you'll get only the happy path.
