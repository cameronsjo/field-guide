Anatomy of a visual prompt

A good description names one or two words from several groups. Here is the kind of sentence that would have landed the Agentic 101 look straight away. Tap any word to jump to its card.

  • Pick one word per slotRendering, color, layout and mood each get one choice. Stacking five rendering styles gives Claude nothing to commit to.
  • Name a reference"Like an IKEA manual" or "like Stripe's docs" carries dozens of decisions. Each card lists a few to borrow.
  • Say what to avoid"No gradients", "no stock-photo people", "no drop shadows". Exclusions narrow things fast.
  • Ask for options"Show me the same diagram in flat, isometric and line art" is a quick way to find the word when you're unsure.
