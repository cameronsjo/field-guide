Voice is easiest to hear when the facts stay fixed. Pick a message and read across: the information never changes, only who seems to be saying it. Tap a voice to open its card.

Anatomy of a voice prompt

A writing prompt that lands names a voice (who is talking), a tone (how they feel right now), a structure (the order things arrive in), a device or two, and something to point at. Here is what that sounds like for three different jobs. Tap any word to jump to its card.

  • Voice stays, tone movesVoice is the personality and shouldn't change between a launch post and an outage notice. Tone is the mood, and should. Name both.
  • Say what it isn't"Witty, not snarky" or "warm, not gushing" does more than another adjective. The Not line on each voice card gives you one.
  • Point at a writer, then describe them"Like Joan Didion: cool, exact, first person, short declaratives" works even if the model's idea of Didion is fuzzy.
  • Give a sample and a word countPaste two sentences you like and say "about 150 words". Length and an example steer harder than any list of adjectives.
