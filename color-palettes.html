Anatomy of a color prompt

A strong color direction names a relationship, a mood, a light and a finish, then gives each color a job. Here is the kind of sentence that describes a screen-print poster of a sailboat heading into a low sun. Tap any word to jump to its card.

  • Relationship before hex“Analogous with one complementary accent” tells Claude how colors relate, so every new color it adds still fits.
  • Give every color a job“Amber for light, teal for shadow, cream for highlights.” Roles stop colors drifting into places they don’t belong.
  • Name the lightGolden hour, overcast and neon night each recolor the whole scene. One light word beats five color words.
  • Borrow a palette“Nord”, “Kodak Portra” or “Wes Anderson pinks” each carry a whole tested palette. Tap a swatch to copy exact hexes when you need them.
